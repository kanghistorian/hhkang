AUDIT LIMITS
Searches used speaker names, shortened and variant titles, host/event names, and event archives. Located pages were checked for embedded images and attachments when accessible; downloaded promotional images were visually inspected. Some hosts returned 403, 404, 410, 429, 502, or challenge pages; these limitations are recorded individually. “Not located” does not mean a flyer never existed. No verified flyer was recovered for the six campus talks. Programs and generic artwork were not silently counted as flyers.

Original files retain their source content. The HSS 2025 document is preliminary. Page numbers in the index are PDF file-page numbers, not necessarily printed page numbers.
